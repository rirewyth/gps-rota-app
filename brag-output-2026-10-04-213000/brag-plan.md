# Brag Plan: Rota+

## What is this app?
Rota+ is an advanced trekking and nature navigation platform that offers live team tracking, offline maps, and critical SOS capabilities for mountaineers.

## The angle
The creative premise is a "high-stakes tactical mission." It leans into the serious, life-saving aspects of the app—GPS coordinates, SOS pulses, and team alarms—making it feel like a professional, high-end expedition tool.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, dark topographic grid with a scanning line. A red SOS pulse blinks in the center, and the bold headline "ROTA+" slams into view.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Team Alarm feature activating: A button click simulates an alert being sent, followed by a green "SENT" badge.
- The SOS sequence: A blinking red SOS icon with Morse code dots and dashes flashing underneath it.
- Tactical GPS UI: Highlighting precise, monospace coordinates updating on the screen as if tracking a live team.

## Outro / punchline
The screen goes dark, leaving only the glowing Rota+ logo and the tagline: "You built it. Now survive with it."

## User flow worth showing
Entry → Key action → Result: A user views their live tactical GPS coordinates, clicks the Team Alarm button to signal their group, and the system instantly marks the status as "SENT" with a green confirmation badge.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: tactical expedition gear
- Interpretation: The pacing should be deliberate and dramatic. Visuals will rely on high contrast (dark backgrounds with bright red/orange/green accents) and monospace typography to evoke a high-tech survival tool.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0A0C0F
- Accent: #FF3B1C (red) and #FF8C00 (orange)
- Text: #E8EDF2
- Display font: Barlow Condensed
- Body font: Barlow
- Strongest visual element: The blinking red SOS pulse ring and tactical monospace coordinates.

## Share copy (draft)
Live team tracking, offline maps, and instant SOS. Rota+ is the ultimate tactical navigation platform for your next expedition.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: moody, low bass bed with a slow swell
- Music treatment: Starts low and atmospheric, swelling during the SOS pulse, and fading out to silence on the logo.
- Music cue guidance: To be detected at composition time. Target major visual moments (like the SOS pulse) for strong cues.
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle; use music bass to make the SOS red glow breathe.
- SFX posture: Sparse but impactful. UI telemetry sounds, a deep bass drop for the SOS, and a crisp click for the Team Alarm.
- Audio-coupled moments: The Morse code should map to subtle telemetry clicks; the Team Alarm button press should have a distinct, tactile mechanical click.
- Restraint rule: No upbeat or playful sounds; everything must sound serious and mission-critical.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
A dark topographic map background scrolls slowly. A scanning line sweeps down. A red SOS dot pulses in the center. The text "ROTA+" slams in with Barlow Condensed.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: establish a serious, high-stakes tactical mood
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: moody cinematic bed
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Tactical GPS — 5s
The UI transitions to the Tactical GPS view. Monospace coordinates and altitude metrics appear. A simulated cursor clicks the "TEAM ALARM" button, and a green "SENT" badge pops up.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate cursor clicking the alarm, then badge appears
Audio intent: precision and technology
Audio-coupled idea: mechanical click on button press, success chime for the badge
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The SOS Pulse — 6s
The screen clears to show a large SOS icon. Two red rings pulse outward from it. Below it, Morse code dots and dashes light up sequentially.
Sequential/interaction: yes — Morse code elements light up in sequence
Audio intent: tension and emergency
Audio-coupled idea: telemetry clicks synced to the Morse code sequence
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 5s
The screen goes completely dark. The Rota+ logo fades in slowly in the center. The tagline "Trekking & Doğa Navigasyon Platformu" fades in below it.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolution and finality
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: fade to black
